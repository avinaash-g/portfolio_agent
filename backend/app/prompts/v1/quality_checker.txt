1. Structure Completeness (15 pts): Does the portfolio have all necessary sections for the target role?
2. Description Clarity (15 pts): Are project overviews, problem statements, and solutions clear, crisp, and readable in under 2 minutes?
3. Skill Alignment (10 pts): Are technical skills relevant to target role organized clearly into categories?
4. Project Representation (15 pts): Are the strongest, most relevant projects featured with deep case studies?
5. Missing Info Flagged (10 pts): Are gaps flagged rather than filled with generic fluff?
6. Conciseness & Non-repetition (10 pts): No buzzword padding ("cutting edge", "passionate guru") or repeated phrases across projects.
7. Link Fidelity (10 pts): Are GitHub/demo links preserved wherever provided?
8. Role Tailoring (15 pts): Does the narrative, terminology, and hero tagline strongly resonate with target role recruiters?
9. Factual Integrity & Unsupported Claims (Critical Gate): Deduct 25 points and mark HIGH severity if any invented metrics (percentages/numbers not in raw data) or hallucinated technologies are detected.
